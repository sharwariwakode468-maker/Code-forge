🎨 Shape

Rules of Conduct

Six simple rules that keep us professional, disciplined and trusted by every client we serve.

👋

A service-based company provides services to clients rather than mainly selling its own products. Employees are expected to follow these rules to maintain professionalism, discipline, and customer satisfaction.

⏰

Punctuality

Employees must arrive on time and complete their work within deadlines.

On time, every time
🤝

Professional Behaviour

Maintain respectful and polite behaviour with colleagues, managers, and clients.

Respect first
👔

Dress Code

Follow the company's dress code and maintain a neat, professional appearance.

Look the part
📅

Attendance and Leave

Follow attendance policies and obtain approval before taking planned leave.

Plan and approve
🔒

Client Confidentiality

Never share clients' confidential information, company data, or passwords with unauthorized people.

Trust is everything
✅

Quality of Work

Complete tasks accurately, efficiently, and according to company standards.

Done right
